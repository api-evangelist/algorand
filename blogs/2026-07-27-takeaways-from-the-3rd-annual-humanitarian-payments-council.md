We recently convened the Humanitarian Payments Council for the third year – this time in Washington D.C.- gathering leaders from humanitarian organizations, governments, payment providers, financial institutions, and technology companies to continue an important conversation: how can stablecoins and other digital payment technologies help humanitarian aid reach people more efficiently, transparently, and securely?
