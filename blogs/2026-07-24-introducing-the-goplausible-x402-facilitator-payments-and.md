The internet was built without a native way to pay. For decades people have worked around that with subscriptions, ad networks, and API keys, none of which were designed for a web where software agents transact on a user’s behalf, or where a single request might be worth a fraction of a cent. GoPlausible 's new x402 Facilitator closes that gap, and it ships a full intelligence dashboard alongside it.
